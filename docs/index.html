Watch together, in sync.

JSync is a player for your own Jellyfin server. Watch movies and shows alone or with friends: pause, seeking and the next episode happen for everyone at the same moment.

  • Netflix-like home, continue watching, next up, my list
  • Watch parties with Jellyfin SyncPlay, invite links and QR codes
  • Skip intro and recap, autoplay next episode, scrub previews
  • AirPlay, Chromecast, picture in picture
  • No JSync account, no servers of ours, no tracking — privacy policy

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. JSync is not affiliated with the Jellyfin project.
